PREFACE
This short book announces to human beings living in the year 2026 that their history will come to an end in 2160.

More precisely, it asserts that humanity’s experience of victory over its fallen state will be completed in that year.

There are therefore 134 years remaining between 2026 and 2160.

The book pursues three objectives:

to present the calculations that lead to the numbers 134 and 2160;

to formulate a warning and an appeal to wisdom;

to invite human beings to wisdom and, if they heed this warning, to joyful expectation.

The argument is based on the biblical numbers associated with days, months, and years.

It also relies on ancient and modern principles according to which there are laws in nature that do not change: time, the constant weight of the Earth, and the conservation of mass, energy, and intelligence.

In human life, nothing is random, trivial, arbitrary, or even sudden; events are merely perceived as such.

This short book is intended to form part of a series devoted to several related subjects. These subjects are related because everything is connected and nothing is the product of chance. As a sage of antiquity asserted, “all is mind.”

Thought, like everything else in this world, obeys laws—the laws of love—which are the same for everything that exists. The intellect belongs to the same world and, in reality, obeys the same laws as matter, just as marble does, for example.

That is why it is said that certain thoughts are carved in marble.

Thought, words, writings, and marble are therefore directly connected in our universe.

Numbers, as well as algebra and geometry, also obey the rules of the universe.

The level of this book does not claim to eliminate noise completely.

Knowledge is always partial.

The task is to separate the signal from the noise by relying on magnitude and reproducibility, in order to arrive at a robust confirmation.

Chapter 1: Days
1.1 The Texts Placed in Relation
Jeremiah 31:31–34 announces that days are coming and that a new covenant is established after these days.

Hosea 6:2 states: “He makes us live in days, and on the third day he raises us up, and we live before him.”

In Jeremiah, the word “day” appears in the plural in the expressions “days” and “these days.” In Hosea, the expression “in days” precedes the mention of “the third day.”

1.2 Proposed Interpretation
A question arises: how many days does the plural “days” refer to here? In the sequence “in days” followed by “the third day,” “days” designates the first two days, since they are followed by a third day.

The text therefore concerns a total of three prophetic days, divided into two groups: two days and one day.

Under this reading, the explanatory translation becomes: “He revives us in two days, and on the third day he raises us up, and we live before him.”

This is, moreover, the generalized translation found in all versions.

Chapter 2: Days and Years
Numbers 14:34 establishes a correspondence between forty days of exploration and forty years of consequences, according to the formula “one year for each day.”

The text thus establishes the following conversion rule:

1 day = 1 year

Revelation 11:9 and 11 also mention three and a half days.

These events refer to the French Revolution, which prohibited religion for three and a half years.

These facts are confirmed by history.

Chapter 3: Noah’s Ark
3.1 The Three Dimensions
Genesis 6:15 attributes three dimensions to the ark: 300 cubits in length, 50 cubits in width, and 30 cubits in height.

These three dimensions correspond to three periods of time:

Dimension of the ark	Number	Proposed temporal correspondence
Length	300 cubits	Total period of 300 years
Width	50 cubits	Jubilee cycle of 50 years
Height	30 cubits	Adult and active generation of 30 years
3.2 The Number 300
Enoch walked with God for 300 years. The length of the ark—300 cubits—therefore corresponds to the same number. Since the ark was the means of salvation for Noah’s family, it also symbolizes the means of salvation for human beings at the time of the end.

Matthew 24 establishes this relationship between the days preceding the flood and the coming of the Son of Man, until the day when Noah entered the ark.

3.3 The Number 50
Leviticus 25:8–10 commands the counting of seven sabbaths of years, that is, seven times seven years. The seven sabbaths of years produce forty-nine years, after which the fiftieth year must be sanctified. The text thus establishes a jubilee cycle of fifty years, adapted to the scale of social and property-related life.

3.4 The Number 30
Luke 3:23 indicates that Jesus was about 30 years old when he began his ministry. The text therefore suggests that he had reached the age of maturity. Matthew 6:27 also associates one cubit with the duration of life. The cubit is thus symbolically connected with stature and, implicitly, with age.

It follows from these symbols that the period of the end times lasts 300 years and is measured in periods of 50 years. Human generations, in their adult and active phase within society, last 30 years, generally from the age of 30 to 60.

Chapter 4: Initial Date of the Calculation
4.1 The Final Period of 150 Years
By combining Hosea 6:2 with Leviticus 25:8–10, the author interprets the three years as three jubilee years. He therefore obtains:

3 × 50 = 150 years

This duration would be the final half of the total period of 300 years, because the expression “he will raise us up and we will live before him” is understood as announcing humanity’s final victory over its fallen state.

4.2 The 1,260 Day-Years
Daniel 7:25 refers to a time, times, and half a time.

This expression is assigned the following value:

1 + 2 + 0.5 = 3.5 time-years

By adopting a prophetic year of 360 days:

3.5 × 360 = 1,260 day-years

Revelation 11:2 mentions 42 months, while Revelation 12:6 mentions 1,260 days. With months of 30 days, the two expressions become equivalent:

42 × 30 = 1,260 days

The number 3.5 thus marks a period of suffering or oppression.

4.3 The Selected Starting Point
The figure described in Daniel 7:25 represents an oppressive power.

This power rises beginning in the year 538, when the Byzantine general Belisarius reconquers Rome and brings the Ostrogothic kingdom to an end.

Another important period, because it leads to the completion of this process, is the establishment of its temporal power, beginning in the middle of the eighth century, around the year 750, and culminating in the Donation of Pepin the Short in 754–756.

The final calculation retains the year 750 as its starting point.

Taking 750 as the initial date and 1,260 years as the duration:

750 + 1,260 = 2010

The final period of 150 years would therefore extend from 2010 to 2160:

2010 + 150 = 2160

The year 2160 is thus announced by the Bible as the year of the end of the world.

Chapter 5: Why 120 and 150 Years?
Genesis 6:3 assigns humanity 120 years. This sentence concerns a humanity whose members were then living for nearly a thousand years; Genesis 9:29 thus assigns Noah 950 years.

It therefore announces a reduction in the lifespan of all humanity.

Only Noah and his family—eight people in total—were saved in the ark.

The difference between the 150 years assigned to the end times and the 120 years associated with the time of Noah is:

150 − 120 = 30 years

Thirty years represent a generation—the generation of the end.

This difference therefore corresponds to the generation that was lacking among the antediluvian human beings.

Genesis 6:11 describes the earth as corrupt and filled with violence. It may be inferred that antediluvian humanity had reached an extreme degree of violence: an additional period of 30 years would not have constituted a genuine opportunity, but would have prolonged the cruel domination of the strong over the weak whom they had completely enslaved.

Genesis 7:24 states: “The waters prevailed over the earth for 150 days.” The number 150 therefore appears once again as the endpoint of the judgment process, in typological relation to the 120 years that, according to this reading, were not put to proper use.
